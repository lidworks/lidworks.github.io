No app? Use Terminal

Three minutes, one command.

Best for display Macs in a store: it installs nothing and cleans up after itself.

  1. Open Terminal. It's in Applications → Utilities, or press ⌘ Space and type “Terminal”.
  2. Paste this and press Return:
    curl -fsSL https://lidworks.github.io/probe.sh | bash
  3. Film the logo when it asks (optional, but it's the most useful part). Point your phone at the back of the lid and record with sound. Your screen will get brighter and dimmer and go almost black for 6 seconds. Everything goes back to normal at the end.
  4. Send us the zip from your Desktop (and the video) at lidworks@proton.me, or DM @Lidworks312 on X.

Bonus, one more minute: when the script asks “Also test whether WipeMode blocks touches?”, type y. It opens our free screen-cleaning app from a temporary folder, you tap around a black screen, and it's deleted afterwards. It tells us whether the touchscreen is blocked while you wipe.

What the script does

Nothing sneaky. Read it first if you like.

  • Saves a list of your Mac's hardware: the same thing ioreg and hidutil show. Serial numbers, UUIDs, MAC addresses and your user name are removed.
  • Steps the screen brightness up and down and darkens the picture for 6 seconds, so we can see what the logo follows.
  • Puts your brightness back, then zips the results on your Desktop.
  • Installs nothing, asks for no password, and uploads nothing. Sending us the zip is up to you.

Prefer to read before running? Download it, look, then run it:

curl -fsSL https://lidworks.github.io/probe.sh -o probe.sh; open -e probe.sh

Then run it with bash probe.sh. Or just view it in your browser.

FAQ

Questions

My screen is still too bright or too dim afterwards.

The script restores your brightness at the end, and also if you stop it with Ctrl-C. If something still looks off, adjust it with the brightness keys — nothing is changed permanently. Then tell us what happened, it helps.

The picture stayed dark.

The darkening is undone as soon as the script's helper exits. Quitting Terminal always resets it. If you ever see this, please send us a note.

I have the regular MacBook Pro (no glowing logo). Can I help?

Yes, a little: run it and send the zip anyway. It gives us a “before” picture to compare against. We can't give out free licenses for these, but we really appreciate it.

Why doesn't Lidlight just exist already?

Apple didn't document how the logo is lit. Lidlight's app is ready; the last part, actually talking to the light, needs a real machine to figure out. That's you.
