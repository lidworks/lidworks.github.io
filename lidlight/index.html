What it does

More than an on/off switch.

Dim it

On a plane, in a dark room, in a meeting. Turn the glow down to a soft hint, or off, with one click from the menu bar.

On a schedule

Lights out from 11 pm to 7 am, automatically. Set it once and forget it.

Breathe

A slow, soft pulse, timed like the sleep light Mac fans still miss.

Focus light

Turn on a Focus and the logo goes dark, so people around you know not to interrupt.

Shortcuts-friendly

Every mode has a link you can trigger from Shortcuts automations, Raycast or a script.

Hands it back

Quit Lidlight and the logo goes straight back to Apple's default. No leftovers.

Status

Built and waiting for the hardware.

Lidlight's app is ready. The last piece is talking to the logo itself, which we can only test on a real PROLED MacBook Pro. We'll be testing on launch day, and we'll only sell it if it truly works. Already have one? Run our 3-minute test and get Lidlight free.

Not sure your Mac has the glowing logo? Only the MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display has it. Other models have a mirror-finish logo that doesn't light up, so Lidlight won't do anything there.
